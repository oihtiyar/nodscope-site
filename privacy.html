Nodscope Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the Nodscope mobile app ("Nodscope", "the app") uses and why. Nodscope is published by Oguz Ihtiyar ("we", "us"). Contact: oihtiyar@gmail.com.

Summary

  • Network scans run on your device, only when you start them.
  • We do not collect, upload or sell your scan results, device lists or network details.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier. See "Advertising" below.

Information the app uses on your device

To work, Nodscope reads the following on your phone. It stays on your phone and is not sent to us:

DataWhy
Wi-Fi name (SSID), local IP address, subnet mask, gatewayShown on the Network screen and used to decide which addresses to scan
Devices found on your network (IP address, host name, response time)Shown in the device list
Ping and port scan resultsShown on screen

Scans send standard network requests (ICMP echo / TCP connection attempts) only to the addresses you scan.

Permissions

  • Local network (iOS): needed to find devices on your Wi-Fi.
  • Location (iOS and Android): the operating system requires it to read the Wi-Fi name. Nodscope does not read or store your location. You can deny it; only the Wi-Fi name will be hidden.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general device information to show and measure ads. On iOS you are asked for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, ads are not personalised. In the EU, UK and other regions where it is required, you are asked for consent before any personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the app settings.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Subscriptions

Nodscope Pro is purchased through the App Store or Google Play. Payments are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active, which processes an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Crash and usage data

Nodscope does not include analytics or crash reporting.

Children

Nodscope is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your rights

Because we do not keep personal data on our servers, there is usually nothing for us to access or delete. For data held by Google (ads), Apple, or RevenueCat, use their tools or contact them. Users in the EU/UK (GDPR) and Turkey (KVKK) can contact us at oihtiyar@gmail.com with any request.

Changes

We will post changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
